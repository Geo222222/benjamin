# The Covenant

The Covenant is Benjamin's constitutional boundary. Product features may evolve; these rules may not be bypassed by convenience, model output, operator intent, or execution pressure.

## COV-001 — Sight is not authority
Epinnox may observe, analyze, rank, forecast, and recommend. Epinnox cannot create an execution authorization.

## COV-002 — Stewardship is explicit
An investment recommendation becomes portfolio intent only through a recorded Steward decision: `APPROVED`, `MODIFIED`, or `REJECTED`.

## COV-003 — The Watchman can stop the Mind
A Steward approval is necessary but insufficient. A Watchman `BLOCK` prevents authorization. There is no ordinary manager-override flag.

## COV-004 — Benjamin does not execute
Benjamin may emit an `AuthorizedExecutionRequest`. It may not call a broker, exchange, wallet, or venue order endpoint.

## COV-005 — The Hand does not think for Benjamin
The Hand may execute the exact authorized instruction or refuse it. It may not originate a trade, increase size, change side, substitute an instrument, or extend an expired authorization.

## COV-006 — Memory is append-only
The Book never mutates or deletes historical truth. Corrections are new entries that cite prior evidence.

## COV-007 — Principal is not spoil
Investor principal, pending subscriptions, and required reserves are not distributable economic gain.

## COV-008 — Distribution follows entitlement
The Portion may distribute only value declared distributable by the accounting/Treasury process and only according to recorded entitlement policy.

## COV-009 — No hidden live mode
Live execution must be an explicit future milestone. This foundation contains no live broker/exchange adapter and Firstfruits investor admission is closed.

## COV-010 — Evidence before claims
Every material state transition must be attributable to a durable receipt or deterministic input. A model's assertion is not authoritative state.

## Constitutional equation

```text
STEWARD_APPROVED + WATCHMAN_BLOCKED = NO AUTHORIZATION

VALID_AUTHORIZATION + THE_HAND = POSSIBLE EXECUTION

NO VALID_AUTHORIZATION = NO EXECUTION
```
