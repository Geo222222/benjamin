# Benjamin Privacy Doctrine

Benjamin is a capital system. Its ability to see and act on private information is part of its economic advantage and fiduciary responsibility. Privacy is therefore constitutional, not cosmetic.

## Default posture

Benjamin information is **private unless a specific rule makes a proof public**.

Typical confidential information includes:

- portfolio positions and cash balances;
- investment theses and model outputs;
- acquisition targets and valuations;
- counterparties and negotiation posture;
- tax and banking information;
- risk assessments;
- unrealized opportunities;
- allocation plans and internal deliberations.

Benjamin must not publish these merely because they are material.

## Proof rule

Material decisions must be provable through the private Big Book. A Benjamin event should record enough to prove:

- what authority acted;
- which policy and prior evidence governed the action;
- what exact underlying record was relied upon by digest/reference;
- what downstream authorization or execution resulted.

The underlying source record belongs in Benjamin's governed data store or The Vault according to policy.

## Little Book rule

Benjamin does not write directly to the public Little Book as part of ordinary portfolio operations.

A public proof requires a separate disclosure decision and should reveal only the claim being verified, for example:

- a public authority credential;
- an intentionally disclosed asset claim;
- a periodic institutional state commitment;
- an externally required attestation.

The public proof must not reveal private portfolio construction, transaction economics, strategy, counterparties, or participant wealth unless that specific disclosure has been authorized.

## Evidence privacy classes

Current Benjamin decision, risk, and execution-authorization proofs default to `CONFIDENTIAL_EVIDENCE` with visibility scoped to Benjamin institutional roles.

Future contribution/distribution events may use `PARTICIPANT_PROOF` where a participant has a legitimate right to verify their own entitlement without receiving visibility into other participants.

`SECRET_REGULATED` information is never sent as raw Book evidence.
