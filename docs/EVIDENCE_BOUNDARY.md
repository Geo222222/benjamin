# Benjamin Evidence Boundary

Benjamin emits or references private decision evidence through **The Book**. It never exposes raw operational/cognitive state directly to the public Little Book.

> **ZLJ sees. Benjamin decides. Watchman governs. The Hand executes. The Book remembers and proves.**

## Target private path

```text
ZLJ qualified intelligence
      |
      +--> private Big Book provenance/evidence
      v
Benjamin decision
      |
      +--> private Big Book decision evidence
      v
Watchman evaluation
      |
      +--> private Big Book authorization/block evidence
      v
Watchman AUTHORIZE | BLOCK
      |
      v
The Hand               (only when authorized)
      |
      +--> private Big Book execution evidence
      v
Outcome / reconciliation
```

The Big Book lineage should be sufficient to prove what material evidence existed, what Benjamin decided, what Watchman permitted or blocked, and what The Hand actually did. It need not expose the full thesis, portfolio state, model internals, feature vectors, private research, or source datasets.

A bridge carries another organ's truth; it does not transfer ownership of that truth.

## Transitional event names

Current foundation code may still use legacy event names such as `BENJAMIN.RISK` and `BENJAMIN.AUTHORIZATION`.

Those names preserve the semantics of existing B1 evidence and must not be silently rewritten. They do **not** mean the target architecture gives Benjamin final governance authority.

Future bridge migration should move target authorization ownership to Watchman while maintaining causal links to historical records.

## Public path

There is no automatic public path.

```text
private institutional fact
      |
      v
explicit disclosure decision
      |
      v
minimum public claim + Big Book commitment
      |
      v
Little Book
```

Examples of permissible public proof classes may include a public authority credential, intentionally disclosed asset claim, or institutional state commitment. Ordinary trading, portfolio, model, decision, governance, and execution records are not public merely because they are material.

## Failure posture

If required private evidence/provenance or Watchman authorization cannot be established, the action does not proceed to The Hand.

Public Little Book availability is not a prerequisite for ordinary private capital operations unless a specific governing policy explicitly requires a public commitment before action.
