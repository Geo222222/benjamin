# Benjamin Evidence Boundary

Benjamin emits proofs to the **Big Book**, never raw operational state to the public Little Book.

## Internal path

```text
Epinnox recommendation
      |
      v
Benjamin decision
      |
      +--> confidential Big Book proof
      v
Watchman result
      |
      +--> confidential Big Book proof
      v
Authorization
      |
      +--> confidential Big Book proof
      v
The Hand
```

The Big Book proof is sufficient to prove lineage and authority. It need not expose the full investment thesis, portfolio state, valuation workbook, negotiation record, or source dataset.

## Public path

There is no automatic public path.

```text
Benjamin private fact
      |
      v
explicit disclosure decision
      |
      v
minimum public claim + Big Book commitment
      |
      v
Little Book
```

Examples of permissible public proof classes may include a public authority credential, intentionally disclosed asset claim, or institutional state commitment. Ordinary portfolio events are not public disclosures.

## Failure posture

If required Big Book proof cannot be produced, Benjamin does not hand a new authorization to The Hand. Public Little Book availability is never a prerequisite for private capital operations unless a specific policy explicitly requires a public commitment before action.
