# Minimum Necessary Evidence

Benjamin must preserve enough proof to establish material capital events without turning the proof layer into a surveillance copy of the capital system.

For each event, ask:

1. What institutional fact must be provable later?
2. Which authority or policy made the event valid?
3. Which source record was relied upon?
4. Which participant or role has a legitimate right to inspect the proof?
5. What information is unnecessary to those purposes?

Only the necessary proof belongs in the Big Book envelope. Full source records remain in Benjamin's governed store or The Vault.

Examples of information that normally stays out of proof envelopes include full bank records, tax identifiers, credentials, full valuation workbooks, complete investment theses, private correspondence, and unrelated portfolio positions.
