Your capital remains your account.

The intended Benjamin client model is not a pooled fund. Benjamin principal capital and another client’s capital remain distinct.

03 / GOVERNANCE

ACCOUNT-BOUNDED AUTONOMY

Autonomy exists inside your mandate.

A Benjamin client does not approve each scalp or intraday action. The client establishes the account relationship and grants a defined mandate. Benjamin may decide only inside that account’s scope, while Watchman evaluates each proposed external action against that account’s current authority and controls.

“Give it to Benjamin” means delegating defined decision authority over designated capital—not surrendering the account, custody, or unlimited control.

THE BOOK

Your account’s decision should be reconstructable after the money moves.

05 / PRIVATE CLIENTS

RELATIONSHIP FIRST

There is no public “sign up.”

Benjamin serves private client relationships one account at a time. An invitation begins a controlled process to establish identity, financial profile and suitability, an account-specific mandate, agreements, an external broker or custodian connection, and funding. The invitation itself opens nothing and grants no authority.

06 / INSTITUTION

BUILT TO BE ACCOUNTABLE

The machine can be fast.
The institution cannot be careless.

01

Account separation

A client account is not pooled with Benjamin principal capital or another client’s account. Mandate, custody, performance, and history remain attributable.

02

Independent custody

Benjamin’s intended trading authority is intentionally distinct from possession or unrestricted withdrawal authority.

03

Governed execution

Benjamin can decide quickly, but an external financial effect occurs only after Watchman governs and The Hand executes within the exact authorization.

04

Evidence before narrative

The Book preserves material lineage so decisions and outcomes can be evaluated without rewriting the past after results are known.
