03 / GOVERNANCE

MANDATED AUTONOMY

Autonomy exists inside authority.

Benjamin may make high-speed decisions, but not outside the client mandate or Watchman’s governing controls. The client does not approve each scalp; the client approves the boundary in which Benjamin may operate.

“Give it to Benjamin” means entrusting designated capital to a defined mandate—not surrendering unlimited control.

06 / INSTITUTION

BUILT TO BE ACCOUNTABLE

The machine can be fast.
The institution cannot be careless.

01

Capital separation

Client capital, account history, mandates, and Benjamin principal capital remain independently attributable.

02

Independent custody

Trading authority is intentionally distinct from possession or withdrawal authority.

03

Governed execution

An intended trade does not become an executed trade until Watchman and The Hand establish that truth.

04

Evidence before narrative

Benjamin explains from qualified records rather than inventing a story after the result is known.
